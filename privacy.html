MyHQ Privacy Notice

What this app is

MyHQ is a personal tool for one user. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

What data it handles

With the owner's permission, it reads transactions and balances from the owner's own bank accounts, through a licensed open-banking provider. It does not read anyone else's accounts.

Where data is kept

Data is stored on the owner's own computer and in encrypted backups on the owner's own drive. It is not sent to any other service, apart from the open-banking provider and any messaging service the owner chooses to use.

What the app does not do

It does not sell data, show advertising, or share data with third parties. It does not log full account numbers, account holder names, or transaction references.

Security

Sensitive fields are encrypted at rest. Secrets are kept outside the code. Access to the app requires a login.

Your rights

The owner is the only user and can access, correct or delete their data at any time. For questions, contact: willian.angelow@gmail.com
